## Lane 1 — Airtasker / Gumtree website-fix and quick-build gigs (Perth)

### What it is

Bid on posted tasks (Airtasker) and/or list services (Gumtree). Target: "simple business website", "WordPress fix", "landing page", "website not working", tradie/concreter/landscaper builds with logo ready.

### Why it fits Caisson

- Buyers already have budget and urgency (posted a task).
- Caisson preview-first workflow beats "send me your ideas" cowboys.
- $180–$250 undercuts typical Airtasker quotes ($200–$1,000) while staying above $50 spam bids.

### First 3 actions

1. **Airtasker Tasker profile** — Name: Edison Rees / Caisson. Bio: Perth web dev, free preview before you pay, one-off pricing from $180, link `edisonrees.github.io/macrosa-web` demos. Photo: real headshot. **Edison approval before publish.**
2. **Saved searches + alerts** — Perth WA: `website`, `wordpress`, `small business website`, `landing page`, `website fix`, `web design`. Check 2x daily (morning + evening).
3. **Three bid templates** (match task type; no em dashes) — **Edison approval before first bid:**
   - **Quick fix** ($80–$120): broken layout, contact form, mobile issue, plugin swap, 1-page tweak.
   - **New small biz site** ($180 Basic / $250 Medium): 4–6 pages, mobile, contact form, preview link in bid.
   - **Redesign / multi-page** ($250–$400): outdated site, new branding, scope in Advanced tier.

**Gumtree (parallel, lower effort):** One listing under Services > Web Development, Perth. Same copy as profile. Refresh weekly. Expect more spam than Airtasker; treat as passive inbound.

### Time estimate

| Activity | Time |
|----------|------|
| Profile + Gumtree listing (once) | 2–3 hours |
| Per bid (customize + attach demo if relevant) | 15–25 min |
| Weekly rhythm (5–8 bids + alerts) | 3–4 hours/week |
| Delivery per closed gig (Basic) | 4–8 hours (existing demo pipeline shortens this) |

### Expected conversion

| Metric | Conservative | Optimistic |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| Bids → offer accepted | 10–15% (new Tasker) | 25–35% (price + preview hook) |
| First 2 weeks (5 bids/week) | 0–1 close | 2–3 closes |
| Revenue per close | $180–$250 | $250–$400 |
| 30-day lane target | $360–$500 | $900–$1,200 |

Assumes competitive bids with **demo link in first message** (differentiator). Tasks budgeting under $100: only take if <1 hour work or skip.

### Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Scope creep ("can you also do SEO/ads/logo") | Fixed tier in bid; Advanced $400 cap; written scope in Airtasker message |
| Payment / dispute | Use Airtasker payment; never start until offer accepted; screenshot scope |
| Race to bottom ($50 Taskers) | Do not bid on "build my shopify store $50" tasks |
| Time sink on custom quotes | Cap at 8 bids/week; reuse templates |
| Gumtree scams | No off-platform payment; Perth meet only if Edison chooses |
| Brand drift | Same Caisson voice; no "AI website" language |

## Lane 2 — LinkedIn DM outreach (Perth tradies / beauty / small biz)

### What it is

Cold/warm DMs to Perth business owners on LinkedIn: plumbers, electricians, mobile mechanics, lash/nail/hair studios, dog groomers, cafes with a company page.

### Why it's weaker than IG/WA/Airtasker

- Most Caisson ICP lives on Instagram, Facebook, or WhatsApp, not LinkedIn.
- Cold B2B benchmarks: ~5–10% DM response when warmed; tradies with empty LI profiles convert near zero.
- LinkedIn penalizes automation and bulk cold DMs.

**Use as optional supplement**, not a replacement for the sprint. Best for: business owners who post on LI, home-service companies with "Director" profiles, beauty studios with professional branding.

### First 3 actions

1. **Edison LinkedIn profile refresh** — Headline: "I build websites for Perth tradies and small businesses | Caisson". Banner: Caisson or Perth local. Featured link: Caisson site + 2 demo links. **Edison approval.**
2. **Target list (50 accounts)** — Suburbs: Joondalup, Rockingham, Mandurah, CBD, northern suburbs. Filter: posted in last 90 days OR company page with <5 employees. Store in `leads/linkedin-targets.csv` (name, company, URL, vertical, demo slug if built).
3. **Engagement week before DM** — 2 thoughtful comments/day on target posts (no pitch). Then DM batch of 5/week using template below. **Edison approval per batch.**

**DM template sketch (no em dashes):**

> Hi [Name], saw [Company] around [suburb]. Quick one from Edison at Caisson in Perth. I build mobile sites for local tradies and salons. Made a free preview for businesses like yours when they don't have a proper site yet. Happy to send a link if useful. No stress if not.

Attach demo only if Macrosa demo exists for similar vertical.

### Time estimate

| Activity | Time |
|----------|------|
| Profile refresh | 1 hour |
| Build 50-target list | 2 hours |
| Daily engagement | 15–20 min/day |
| 5 DMs/week (personalized) | 45 min/week |
| **Weekly total** | ~3 hours |

### Expected conversion

| Metric | Conservative | Optimistic |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| DM response rate (cold, local SMB) | 3–5% | 8–12% (warm after comments) |
| Response → paid | 10–20% | 25–35% |
| 20 DMs/month | 0–1 close | 1 close |
| 30-day lane target | $0–$180 | $180–$250 |

Lower than Airtasker and IG/WA. Only worth it if Edison wants LI credibility long-term.

### Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Account restriction | Max 5 DMs/week; no automation tools; engagement before DM |
| Wrong audience | Skip profiles with no activity in 12 months |
| Off-brand (too corporate) | Match Caisson plain tone; link demos not PDF decks |
| Time cost | Kill lane if 0 responses after 30 DMs |

### Edison approval before send?

**Yes.** Profile changes, target list sample (10 rows), and every DM batch before send. No exceptions.

## Lane 3 — Google Business Profile setup / optimization (upsell)

### What it is

Not a separate outbound channel. **Upsell** on Macrosa outreach, Airtasker bids, and reply follow-ups. One-time setup: verify profile, categories, description, services, photos, hours, website link, first Google post.

### Why it fits

- Medium tier already includes "Google indexing"; GBP is the practical delivery customers understand ("show up on Maps").
- Market one-time setup: ~$100–$149 AUD (Nerds2U $100, My SEO Mate $149). Caisson **$80 add-on** or bundle into Medium ($250) reads as value.
- GBP leads convert strongly for local services (~42% call conversion benchmark for home services). Helps clients see ROI, which drives referrals.

### First 3 actions

1. **Internal GBP checklist + SOW** — Create `leads/gbp-setup-checklist.md`: verification steps, category rules, photo specs, what Caisson does vs client (client: business access, ABN, photos; Caisson: setup, copy, linking). **No customer send.**
2. **Upsell line for WA/IG follow-ups** — Draft 2 variants for Edison approval:
   - Soft: "If you want to show on Google Maps too, I can set up your Business Profile for $80 one-off."
   - Bundle: "Medium at $250 includes your site live, Google indexing, and full Business Profile setup."
3. **Flag pipeline leads** — In `pipeline.md`, tag leads with no GBP or unverified listing as `GBP upsell` when replying to interest ("yes send link" messages).

### Time estimate

| Activity | Time |
|----------|------|
| Checklist + SOW (once) | 1–2 hours |
| Per client setup (after sale) | 30–60 min |
| Pitch line in follow-up | +0 (part of existing reply) |

### Expected conversion

| Metric | Conservative | Optimistic |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| Upsell on closed site deal | 30% take $80 add-on | 50% |
| Lift on fence-sitters (site only) | +5% close to Medium | +10% close |
| 10 interested replies/month | +$80–$160 | +$200–$400 |

GBP does not replace site sale; it **increases Medium uptake** and **adds margin on wins**.

### Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Verification delay (postcard/video) | Set expectation: setup starts after verification; don't charge until profile live or split $40 start / $40 live |
| Wrong primary category | Use Google's category picker; one primary only |
| Client won't grant Manager access | Send step-by-step; Edison does live call if needed |
| Ranking promises | Never guarantee #1; say "helps locals find you on Maps" |
| Ongoing management scope creep | One-time only unless Edison launches retainer later |

### Edison approval before send?

**Yes** for any new customer copy (follow-up lines, Airtasker mention of GBP).  
**No** for internal checklist.  
**No** for delivery after client has paid (ops, not outreach).
