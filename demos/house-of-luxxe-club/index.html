Show this code when you come in.
  1. Join once. Thirty dollars a month, cancel whenever you like.
  2. You get a member code. It lives on your phone.
  3. Order what you want, show the code, thirty dollars comes off.
  4. Staff mark it claimed so it cannot be used twice in the same month.
  5. On the first of the month your credit goes back to thirty dollars.
This is a working prototype, not a live product. Membership state is stored in this browser only, so you can test the whole flow including the monthly reset without anything being charged or sent anywhere.
