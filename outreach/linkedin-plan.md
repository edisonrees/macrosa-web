## 1. Target profile criteria

### Who to message

- **Owner, founder, director, or sole trader** at a small business (1–20 staff). Skip corporate marketing managers at big chains.
- **Decision-maker signals:** "Owner at …", "Founder", "Managing Director", "Principal", "Self-employed", "Freelance", "Contractor".
- **Secondary titles worth a look:** Salon manager, studio owner, shop owner, tradie running their own van/crew.

### Industries (Perth focus)

| Priority | Industries | Why |
|----------|------------|-----|
| High | Hair, lashes, brows, nails, beauty, barber | Often IG/Fresha-only; Medium tier ($250) angle |
| High | Plumbing, electrical, HVAC, painting, landscaping, pressure cleaning | Tradies with weak or no site; Basic tier ($180) |
| High | Mobile mechanic, auto detail, dog grooming | Local search matters; Basic tier |
| Medium | Cafes, catering, home bakeries, food trucks | Menu/enquiry site; Medium tier |
| Medium | Cleaning, gardening, handyman, fencing | Facebook-only leads; Basic tier |
| Skip | Agencies, SaaS, recruiters, franchises, government | Wrong buyer or no budget fit |

### Perth geo signals (must match at least one)

- **Location field:** Perth, Western Australia; Greater Perth; or a Perth suburb (Joondalup, Rockingham, Fremantle, Mandurah, Ellenbrook, etc.).
- **About / headline:** "Perth", "WA", "Western Australia", suburb name, or "servicing Perth metro / Peel / northern suburbs".
- **Company page:** Australian address in Perth metro; `.com.au` site missing or clearly outdated.
- **Posts:** Local job photos, suburb tags, Perth event mentions.

### Disqualifiers

- Already has a polished custom site (not a Wix/Facebook link buried in bio).
- On banlist or prior outreach in Macrosa `outreach/` (no double-tap).
- Enterprise (50+ employees), national franchise HQ, or obvious agency selling the same services.
- Profile inactive 12+ months (no posts, stale "open to work" only).

### Pre-send checklist (per prospect)

1. Confirm Perth/WA geo.
2. Confirm owner or operator (not employee).
3. Build demo slug if none exists: `https://edisonrees.github.io/macrosa-web/demos/{slug}/ `
4. Pick tier: Basic ($180) for tradies/mobile; Medium ($250) for beauty/food with SEO/domain angle.
5. Queue draft to Discord; wait for Edison **approve** before any send.

## 5. Daily volume (LinkedIn limits)

LinkedIn punishes bursts. Stay human-paced.

| Action | Recommended daily cap | Notes |
|--------|----------------------|-------|
| **Profile views** | 40–60 | Spread across the day; don't script-click |
| **Connection requests** | 10–15 | Blank or 1 short line; no URL in invite |
| **New DMs (1st degree)** | 8–12 | Primary outreach lane |
| **Follow-ups** | 3–5 | Only 3+ days after opener, max 1 bump |
| **Weekly total new conversations** | ~40–50 | Back off if warnings or low accept rate |

**Weekly rhythm**

- **Mon–Thu:** New prospects + same-day Discord approvals from Edison.
- **Fri:** Follow-ups only; no new cold blitz.
- **Sat–Sun:** Off or 2–3 follow-ups max.

**Hard stops**

- Any LinkedIn restriction warning: pause 48 hours.
- Accept rate under ~30% on invites: drop to 5 invites/day and soften invite note.
- Never automate sends; never admit AI; never use phone/SMS/FB for this lane.
